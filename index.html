Help

The city doesn't load. Apple Maps 3D cities need an internet connection. Check Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again.

Where are my purchases? Open the Shop (bag icon on the main menu or in the Garage) and tap Restore purchase. Purchases also come back automatically on every device signed in to the same Apple Account.

Leaderboards don't open. Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then reopen the game.

Controls. Settings lets you switch stick layout (Mode 1 or Mode 2), left-handed buttons, sensitivity and dead zone. Game controllers work too.

Daily reminder. Turn it on or off in Settings; it only ever sends one reminder after you stop playing.
