Privacy Policy

SkyWorks Drone Pilot · Effective October 6, 2026
SupportPrivacy Policy

In short: the developer collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress (money, drones, records, settings) is saved only on your iPhone or iPad. Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple services the game uses

  • Apple Maps shows the 3D cities.
  • WeatherKit (Apple Weather): when you pick a launch point with LIVE weather on, its coordinates are sent to Apple to get the current weather there. It is not your own location.
  • Game Center: if you are signed in, your time-trial times and pilot XP are submitted to Game Center leaderboards.
  • In-App Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details.

Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Map data services

To make buildings and hills solid, the game downloads map tiles for the area you fly in from OpenFreeMap (buildings) and the public Terrain Tiles dataset on Amazon Web Services (elevation). Like any website, these servers see your IP address and which map area was requested. Nothing that identifies you is sent.

Permissions

  • Photos (add only): to save photos and clips your drone takes, when you choose to.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder. They are scheduled on your device.
  • Motion: the title screen uses the phone's tilt for a parallax effect. It never leaves the device.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: contact the developer.
